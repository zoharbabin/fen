Fen privacy policy

Fen collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We run no server that receives anything from the app.

Where your files live

Fen reads and writes only the documents you open and the folders you add as workspaces. Its own data (search index, settings, activity log) stays on your device in the .fen folder of your home directory, or in the app container for the Mac App Store build. If a file sits in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs it under your Apple Account. Fen does not see that traffic.

Connections you can start

Fen connects to the network only for these features. Each one is off until you use it.

API keys and tokens are stored in the system Keychain, not in your documents.

Share extension

The iOS share extension saves the text you share into a folder shared with the Fen app on your device. It makes no network connection.

Children

Fen collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Fen is open source. This page changes only with the code, and the history is in the repository. Ask questions or report a privacy concern in the issue tracker.
