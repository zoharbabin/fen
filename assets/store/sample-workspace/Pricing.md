# Pricing

We moved to a yearly plan. See [[Launch plan]] for the date and [[Interview notes]] for why.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | Trail search, 3 offline maps |
| Plus | $24 / year | Unlimited offline maps |
| Family | $39 / year | Plus for up to 5 people |

## Break-even

With churn $c$ per month and a yearly price $P$, the average customer pays

$$
\text{LTV} = \frac{P}{12c}
$$

At $P = 24$ and $c = 0.05$, that is $\text{LTV} = 40$ dollars.
