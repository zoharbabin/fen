# Interview notes

Eight hikers, 30 minutes each, in September.

## What we heard

1. Maps must work with no signal. Six of eight said this first.
2. Subscriptions feel risky. A **yearly** price felt fair to most.
3. Nobody used the social feed.

> "I don't open the app until I'm at the trailhead, and then I need it to work."

Feeds into [[Pricing]] and the [[Roadmap]].
